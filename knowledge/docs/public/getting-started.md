This page shows how to start a first collection and what each kind of user sees in Knowledge. It is for anyone opening
Knowledge for the first time, and for AI agents working out what a person can do.

## Before you start

Sign up on insulinapps.com with an e-mail address, Google or your company's single sign-on, then create or join an
organization. Owners and admins invite the team and give each person a role: Admin, Editor or Viewer. See
[Organizations and roles](/docs/platform/organizations-and-roles). Then open Knowledge from your apps.

## Start your first collection

Admins and Editors in Knowledge can start collections. **New collection** takes five steps:

1. **What it is for.** Give it a name and say, in one sentence, what it is for. AI assistants read that sentence to
   decide when to use the collection. It is private to you, its owner, until you share it.
2. **Add what you know.** Upload files, add a web page or write a note. Files Insulin can't read are named, with the
   reason, before anything starts.
3. **Learning.** Each file goes Waiting → Reading → Understanding → learned, with the time left. You can leave the
   page; Knowledge e-mails you when it is done.
4. **What it learned.** Check the facts it found: **Looks right** marks one as checked, **Fix** corrects it. Then try a
   question: pick one of up to three made from those facts (each reads "… — is that right?") to ask it and see the
   answer with its sources, or type your own.
5. **Share it.** Share it with everyone in your organization or with specific people, and choose whether AI assistants
   and API clients can ask it.

Then ask it on its **Ask** tab, or from **Home** across up to five collections at once.

## What each role sees

Knowledge uses each person's role in Knowledge from insulinapps.com: from their organization role (organization owners
and admins are Admins here), or a role given for Knowledge alone.

| Role in Knowledge | Navigation | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| Viewer | Home, Collections, Docs, Settings | Ask the collections shared with them, open sources, see what a collection knows and its history, and report a wrong answer. They never change anything, whatever role a collection gives them. |
| Editor | Also **Needs attention** | Start collections, and change the collections where their collection role allows it. |
| Admin | Also **Rules and data** and **Health**, under Admin | Everything an Editor does, plus what is hidden before learning, who writes answers, usage beside the limits and the health of the collections they have a role in. |

Being an Admin in Knowledge gives no access to any collection's content: inside a collection, what someone can do
comes from their collection role. Owners of the organization also see the basic information of every collection and
can delete one at a time. See [Roles and sharing](/docs/knowledge/roles-and-sharing).

**Settings** in Knowledge's navigation opens Insulin Apps' pages for Knowledge on insulinapps.com: People,
Integrations, API access and Connect AI.

## A collection's tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Overview | What it covers, its state in plain words (ready, asleep, learning…), and where people ask it |
| Ask | Answers with numbered sources, or "I won't guess"; fix or report an answer |
| Sources | Files, web pages and notes, with their learning progress; stop using or remove one |
| What it knows | Facts as sentences, each with its file: Looks right, Fix, Report a problem |
| Sharing | People and general access, AI assistants and API clients, keep it ready, export, transfer, delete |
| History | Restore points: go back to one, and undo |

**Needs attention**, for Editors and Admins, lists files Insulin couldn't read, values hidden before learning,
reported answers waiting and corrections to check, across the collections they can change.
