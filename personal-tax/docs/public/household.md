The household step records who is in the household and the facts no document shows. It is the first step of every tax
year. This page explains each part of it.

## Start a tax year

Open **Returns** and choose **Start 2025**. The year opens on its household step. Someone with the Viewer role sees the
year but can't start it.

## Add the people

Start with yourself: the person preparing the returns. Then add the others who live with you:

- **Spouse or partner:** your spouse or common-law partner. There can be one.
- **Child** or **other dependant:** someone you support, such as a child or a parent who lives with you.

Enter each person's name and birth date. Use the names as they appear on their tax slips: AI will use them to decide
whose document each file is. People stay in the household from one year to the next. You can't remove yourself, and you
can't change who "you" are.

## Marital status on December 31

Choose your status on the last day of the year, as the T1 asks it: married, living common-law, widowed, divorced,
separated or single. If it changed during the year, the status on December 31 counts.

## Prepare both returns together

When you are married or living common-law and your spouse or partner is in the household, the app asks whether to
prepare both returns together. Each of you still files your own return. Together, the app can decide who claims shared
credits, such as donations and medical expenses, so the household pays less. Choose "Only" and your name to prepare your
own return alone.

## Province and residency

For each person whose return is prepared, choose the province they lived in on December 31, and say whether they lived
in Canada all year. Personal Tax prepares returns for Ontario, British Columbia and Alberta, for people who lived in
Canada all year. For any other province, or for someone who moved to or from Canada during the year, the page says the
return needs other software.

## Save the step

People are saved as you add or change them. The rest is saved with **Save** or **Save and continue**. "Before you
continue" lists what is still missing; when nothing is, the step shows a check mark.

If someone else changed the same year while you were editing, your save is refused and their changes are kept. Choose
**Reload** to see them, then make your changes again.
