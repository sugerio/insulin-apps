The household is the people a return is prepared for: the same people every year. Each tax year then says who is in
it, and its first step records the facts no document shows. This page explains each part.

## Add the people

Open **Household** in the navigation. Start with yourself: the person preparing the returns. Then add the others who
live with you:

- **Spouse or partner:** your spouse or common-law partner. There can be one.
- **Child** or **other dependant:** someone you support, such as a child or a parent who lives with you.

Enter each person's name and birth date. Use the names as they appear on their tax slips: AI will use them to decide
whose document each file is. The people stay the same from one year to the next. You can't remove yourself, and you
can't change who "you" are. Someone with the Viewer role sees the people but can't change them.

## Start a tax year: who is in it

Open **Returns** and choose **Start 2025**. The app lists everyone in the household with a box to tick:

- **You** are always in your own year.
- **Your spouse or partner:** tick them to prepare both returns together. Each of you still files your own return;
  together, the app can decide who claims shared credits, such as donations and medical expenses, so the household pays
  less. Leave them unticked to prepare only your own return.
- **Children and other dependants:** tick those who lived with you that year.

If someone is missing, choose **Add a new person**: it opens the Household page, and **Back to Start 2025** returns to
the list. To change who is in a year later, choose **Change** in the **Personal info** step of either return.

## Marital status on December 31

Choose your status on the last day of the year, as the T1 asks it. If it changed during the year, the status on
December 31 counts.

The choices follow the Household. With a spouse or partner there, choose married or living common-law, even if their
return isn't prepared this year. Without one, choose single, widowed, divorced or separated. If you add or remove a
spouse later, the year asks for the status again.

The tax treats married and common-law couples the same; the T1 only has a separate box for each. The app doesn't
prepare the return for the year of a separation yet.

## Province and residency

On each return, choose the province the person lived in on December 31, and say whether they lived in Canada all
year. Taxi prepares returns for Ontario, British Columbia and Alberta, for people who lived
in Canada all year. For any other province, or for someone who moved to or from Canada during the year, the page says
the return needs other software.

## Save the step

People are saved as you add or change them, and who is in the year with **Save people**. The year's facts are saved
with **Save** or **Save and continue**. If something is still missing, **Save and continue** saves what you entered and
lists what is left. When nothing is missing, the step shows a check mark.

If someone else changed the same year while you were editing, your save is refused and their changes are kept. Choose
**Reload** to see them, then make your changes again.
