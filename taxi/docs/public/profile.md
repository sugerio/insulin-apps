Your profile is the people a return is prepared for: you, your spouse or partner, and your dependants. They are the same people every year. Each tax year then says who is in
it, and its first step records the facts no document shows. This page explains each part.

## Add the people

Open **Profile** in the navigation. Start with yourself: the person preparing the returns. Then add the others who
live with you:

- **Spouse or partner:** your spouse or common-law partner. There can be one.
- **Child** or **other dependant:** someone you support, such as a child or a parent who lives with you.

Enter each person's name and birth date. Use the names as they appear on their tax slips: AI will use them to decide
whose document each file is. The people stay the same from one year to the next. You can't remove yourself, and you
can't change who "you" are. Someone with the Viewer role sees the people but can't change them.

**Remove** asks first: removing someone deletes their facts, answers and slips (confirmed ones too) in every tax year
they are in. Files uploaded in their return stay in the year. To leave someone out of one year only, change who is in
that year instead.

## Start a tax year: who is in it

Open **Returns** and choose **Start 2025**. The app lists everyone in the profile with a box to tick. Only you are ticked to begin with:

- **You** are always in your own year.
- **Your spouse or partner:** tick them to prepare both returns together. Each of you still files your own return;
  together, the app can decide who claims shared credits, such as donations and medical expenses, so the two of you pay
  less. Leave them unticked to prepare only your own return.
- **Children and other dependants:** tick those who lived with you that year.

If someone is missing, choose **Add a new person**: it opens the Profile page, and **Back to Start 2025** returns to
the list. To change who is in a year later, choose **Change** under **People in 2025** on **Returns**. Someone taken out of a
year loses their facts, answers and slips for that year.

## Marital status on December 31

Choose your status on the last day of the year, as the T1 asks it. If it changed during the year, the status on
December 31 counts.

The choices follow the Profile. With a spouse or partner there, choose married or living common-law, even if their
return isn't prepared this year. Without one, choose single, widowed, divorced or separated. If you add or remove a
spouse later, the year asks for the status again.

The tax treats married and common-law couples the same; the T1 only has a separate box for each. The app doesn't
prepare the return for the year of a separation yet.

## Province and residency

On each return, choose the province the person lived in on December 31, and say whether they lived in Canada all
year. Taxi prepares returns for every province and territory. A resident of Quebec gets two returns: the federal one
and Revenu Québec's TP-1.

If the person moved to or from Canada during the year, answer No. Personal info then asks for the days they lived in
Canada and their income for the rest of the year. Taxi reduces the personal amounts (such as the basic personal amount)
by the days in Canada, unless they had income for the rest of the year and at least 90% of it came from Canada. Taxi
doesn't prepare a Quebec return (TP-1) for the year of a move yet: it says so on that return.

The spouse amount and similar credits go down by the spouse's net income for the days the person lived in Canada. If
the person lived here all year, that is the spouse's net world income for the year. If the person moved and the
spouse's return is prepared here too, Personal info asks whether the spouse lived in Canada on the same days (Taxi then
works it out from the spouse's return) and, if not, the spouse's net world income on the person's days. When the
spouse's return isn't prepared here, it asks the spouse's net world income for the year, and for the person's days too
if the person moved. Credits that depend on family income use both spouses' net incomes for the year.

## Save the step

People are saved as you add or change them, and who is in the year with **Save people**. The year's facts are saved
with **Save** or **Save and continue**. If something is still missing, **Save and continue** saves what you entered and
lists what is left. When nothing is missing, the step shows a check mark. While it saves, the step can't be changed. If
an answer doesn't fit, such as part of a day, the answers aren't saved and the reason shows under it.

If someone else changed the same year while you were editing, your save is refused and their changes are kept. Choose
**Reload** to see them: what you typed stays, except where they changed the same answer (theirs shows), and you can save
again. If the year can't be loaded, the page stays as it was and says so, with a way to try again.
